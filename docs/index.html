The Ceremony (Yes, Really)

The ritual, in four easy steps

One gate contract, zero high priests. The token stays a plain NEP-141 — the magic lives in the gate, so every wallet and tool keeps working. Bring your own candle.

1

The window

Every day, 16:00–20:00 UTC, the RitualGate wakes up and lights the candle. Outside those hours? It accepts nothing at all. Not a crumb. Not a vibe.

2

The offering

You send RITUAL through the gate, naming your real recipient. 97% arrives — 3% stays in the community pot. A tithe, but make it on-chain. The counter ticks up by one.

3

The count

Ten offerings complete the count. At the 10th transfer the release is armed — 30 RITUAL sitting in the pot of a full cycle, patiently glowing.

4

The release

Anyone calls release_pot() and the whole pot lands in the community treasury. No keys, no admin, no trust — just the count. Democracy, but for pot release.
